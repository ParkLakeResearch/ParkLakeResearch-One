About
Park Lake
Research

Park Lake Research is an independent market research platform focused on commercial real estate dynamics along Florida's Space Coast — one of the most consequential growth corridors in the American Southeast.

We publish original analysis on industrial, flex, and land markets, with a practitioner's eye toward supply constraints, aerospace-driven demand, and the economics of ground-up development.

Our work draws on primary broker intelligence, site-level underwriting, and direct engagement with the deals shaping Brevard County's built environment. No intermediary. No noise. Just the research.
