What is Park Lake Research?

Park Lake Research is an independent market research and commentary publication focused on commercial real estate — built from the ground up, from the perspective of someone who actually builds things.

This is not institutional analysis filtered through a $500M fund's viewpoint. It's ground-level notes on what's happening in specific submarkets, what the numbers actually say, and what it means for operators, developers, and investors working at the deal level.

Coverage spans small-bay flex industrial, IOS, mixed-use, and capital markets themes — with a strong Florida focus and a national eye for trends that eventually reach secondary markets.

Primary Focus
Brevard County, FL and the Space Coast — with coverage extending across Florida's secondary and tertiary markets as deals and trends warrant.
Asset Classes
Small-bay flex industrial · Industrial outdoor storage (IOS) · NNN sale-leaseback · Mixed-use development · Capital markets
Perspective
Written by a Florida-licensed certified building contractor and developer. The analysis here is practical, numbers-first, and skeptical of institutional framing applied to small-deal realities.
